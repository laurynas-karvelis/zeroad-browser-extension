# Changelog

All notable changes to the Zero Ad Network browser extension. Versions follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## 1.0.1 - 2026-09-27

### Changed

- **Turning Freedom off now also pauses website discovery and visit measurement.**
  Before, it only stopped membership proofs, and visits kept being measured. The
  visit in focus is counted up to the moment you switch off. Time spent while off
  isn't counted. Visits recorded earlier still upload.
- Popup copy updates.
- Sharper toolbar and extension icons.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-26

First stable release, for Chrome and Firefox.

### Added

- Connect a Freedom membership from the zeroad.network dashboard.
- Send a membership proof to recognized partner websites, over HTTPS only. The
  proof is bound to the website and contains no account ID, name or email.
- Discover participating websites and creator pages from their Publisher ID.
- Measure time on recognized websites and creator pages while the tab and window
  are in focus, and upload activity linked to your account.
- **Turn Freedom off** to stop requesting subscriber benefits on all websites.
- **Report a site issue** from the popup.
- Test access for partner-owned websites and the demo, without losing your
  membership. Test visits don't count towards publisher earnings.
